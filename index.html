Afterwards

Join the tour we have arranged, of the magnificent house upstairs and down. Alternatively enjoy an afternoon strole through the pathways of the Parkland, from right outside the door, all the way down to the River Liffey. Lady Louisa created the Castletown parkland landscape in the ‘natural’ style championed by Capability Brown. The parkland includes meadows, waterways and woodlands with man-made accents carefully inserted into nature for the walker to discover and enjoy: a classical temple, a gothic lodge, a folly, clusters of once rare imported trees dotting wide open spaces, still ponds, cascades and watercourses.
