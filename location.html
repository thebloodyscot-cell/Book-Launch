Location & Accessibility

We hope you enjoy your visit and perhaps take a strole throught the parklands or join our tour of the house after the launch.

Address

Castletown House & Parklands
Celbridge, Co. Kildare
W23 V9H3, Ireland

Open in Google Maps

By foot

From Cebridge Main Street, you can walk striaght through the main gate and onto Castletowns long straight avenue, about 3/4 of a Mile to the main house, which takes only 10 minutes

Important:Access through the main gate is only by foot

By car

Castletown is easily accessible by car, just 20km from Dublin City Centre and 28km from Dublin Airport. Castletown is reached from the M4 via Exit 6 / Celbridge West. Keep left at the exit

Important:Continue along the Barnhall Road, the entrance is to the right hand side. This takes you directly to the Car Park

Car Parking

Please use the official car park, accessed from the Barnhall Road (M4 via Exit 6), located about 80 m from the house itself

Important:There is limited number of disabled parking spots at the Kitchen Wing/Café, just through the archway to the right of the house

Disabled access

Castletown has a wheelchair-accessible toilet and lift among its facilities and Guide dogs are welcome

Important:Please note there is limited accessibility of the museum rooms should you wish to join the tour after the launch, and no wheelchair access to the first or second floor of the main house or the Hunting Room in the Stable Wing.

The restored historic pathways from the front of the house to the river Liffey are wheelchair accessible.

Prams and buggies are not allowed in the museum rooms and must be left at the front or near reception.

Other accessibility varies within the historic house, so please contact the venue in advance if you have any other access requirements

Public transport

Bus connections from Dublin to Celbridge and onward connections from Hazelhatch rail station

Bus: C4 to Celbridge village. The Castletown front gate is near stop 3907. Route 120 also serves English Row (bus stop 7085)

Train: From Hazelhatch rail station, take the W61 bus to Main Street Celbridge, stop number 3907 and the Castletown gate is right in front of you
