Location & Access

Everything you need for your visit to Castletown House.

Address

Open in Google Maps

By car

Castletown is reached from the M4 via Exit 6 / Celbridge West. The venue has on-site car parking.

Important: access arrangements can change. Please check the official Castletown House information before setting out.

Parking

The site lists a car park. Earlier OPW visitor information also notes designated disabled parking at the Kitchen Wing/Café side of the house.

For the latest parking arrangements on the day, contact Castletown directly.

Disabled access

Castletown lists a wheelchair-accessible toilet and lift among its facilities. Accessibility varies within the historic house, so please contact the venue in advance if you have specific access requirements.

Public transport

The official Castletown information notes bus connections from Dublin to Celbridge and onward connections from Hazelhatch rail station.

Bus: C4 to Celbridge village; the Castletown gates are near stop 3907. Route 120 serves English Row (stop 7085).
