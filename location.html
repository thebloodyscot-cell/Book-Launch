Location & Access

Everything you need for your visit to Castletown House. Do enjoy your visit and perhaps take a strole throught the parklands after the launch. They are open all year round and free to visit. The restored historic pathways from the front of the house to the river Liffey are wheelchair accessible. They are mostly on the level with one area of steep incline and some sections of uneven surface.

By foot

From Cebridge Main Street, you can walk striaght through the main gate and onto Castletowns long straight avenue for about 3/4 of a Mile, which takes only 10 minutes.

Important:Access through the main gate is only by foot.

By car

Castletown is easily accessible by car and is only 10 minutes away from Liffey Valley Shopping Centre and the M50. It is just 20km from Dublin City Centre and 28km from Dublin Airport.

Important:Castletown is reached from the M4 via Exit 6 / Celbridge West. Keep left at the exit. Continue along the Barnhall Road, the entrance is on your right hand side.

Car Park

Please use the official car park, accessed from the Barnhall Road (M4 via Exit 6), located about 80 m from the house itself. There is limited number of disabled parking spots at the Kitchen Wing/Café, just through the archway to the right of the house.

fff

Disabled access

Castletown has a wheelchair-accessible toilet and lift among its facilities. Please note there is limited accessibility of the museum rooms should you wish to join the tour while visiting, and no wheelchair access to the first or second floor of the main house or the Hunting Room in the Stable Wing. Prams and buggies are not allowed in the museum rooms and must be left at the front or near reception. Other accessibility varies within the historic house, so please contact the venue in advance if you have any other access requirements. Guide dogs are welcome.

Public transport

Bus connections from Dublin to Celbridge and onward connections from Hazelhatch rail station.

Bus: C4 to Celbridge village; the Castletown front gate is near stop 3907. Route 120 also serves English Row (bus stop 7085).

Train: From Hazelhatch rail station, take the W61 bus to Main Street Celbridge, stop number 3907 and the Castletown gate is right in front of you.
